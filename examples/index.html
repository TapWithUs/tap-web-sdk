Web Bluetooth needs a click, Chrome or Edge, and HTTPS or localhost

Call connect() from a button click. Use Chrome or Edge. Connect reconnects the Tap you last used in this page, else opens the picker. Choose device… always opens the picker. The page must be HTTPS or localhost. Safari and Firefox do not support Web Bluetooth. connect() returns TapSDKWeb or TapSDKWeb2 after firmware detect.
